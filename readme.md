# Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn behavior in a telecom company using Python and exploratory data analysis (EDA) techniques.

The objective is to identify factors influencing customer churn and generate actionable business insights for improving customer retention.

---

## Business Problem
Customer churn directly impacts revenue and business growth.

Understanding why customers leave can help companies:
- improve retention strategies
- reduce customer loss
- increase long-term profitability

---

## Key Insights
- Month-to-month contracts show significantly higher churn rates.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.
- Higher monthly charges may contribute to customer attrition.
- Long-term customer relationships improve retention.

---

## Business Recommendations
- Encourage long-term contract adoption.
- Create retention campaigns for new customers.
- Improve customer experience for high-risk churn groups.
- Optimize pricing and loyalty programs.
